<objective>
Make the workflow release-safe: `analyze`/proposal merges must not erase compiled runtime state, and `migrate` must not silently extract zero rows when `compile` has not produced the required blocks.

User-facing outcome: operators get a clear, actionable failure instead of a green no-op.
</objective>

<scope>
In scope:
- Preserve non-`proposals` top-level mapping blocks during `MappingFile::merge()` per D-12.
- Add a structural compiled-block preflight to `MigrateController::actionIndex()` and `actionLoad()` per D-10 and D-11.
- Test the exact release-readiness regressions.

Non-goals:
- Do not auto-run compile inside migrate.
- Do not create multiple mapping files.
- Do not change mapping row identity semantics except preserving sibling blocks.
</scope>
