<objective>
Create the critical Page-rooted introspection audit: Kunstmaan Page is the source entry point, Craft Entry is the result, and every page-owned related surface is accounted for.

User-facing outcome: operators can inspect a diffable report that explains what will migrate, what was dropped, what is unsupported, and what is out of scope.
</objective>

<scope>
In scope:
- Deterministic coverage rows per accepted Page/root per D-03, D-05, D-06.
- Categories: `migrated`, `dropped`, `out_of_scope`, `unsupported`, `warning`.
- Related surfaces: direct fields, pageparts, implicit content, ManyToOne, ManyToMany, OneToMany, assets, taxonomy/dataProviders/leaf entities, SEO/redirects, CKEditor `[M]`/`[NT]`/media URL references. Each surface must have a named discovery input/service or be explicitly classified as `out_of_scope`, `unsupported`, or `warning`.
- JSON and Markdown artifacts written atomically during compile.

Non-goals:
- Do not copy proprietary content bodies into committed artifacts.
- Do not migrate out-of-scope Kunstmaan subsystems.
</scope>
